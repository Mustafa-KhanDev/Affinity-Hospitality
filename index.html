About Affinity

Affinity Hospitality UAE (also searched as Affinity Hospitality AE) is a leading manufacturer and supplier of high-end bath and bed linen, along with custom branded bags for hospitality and promotional use.

With over two decades of experience, we manufacture premium-quality linen for renowned hotel brands across the GCC and Africa. Alongside our core linen programs, we also produce non-woven, cotton, and polyester bags tailored for hotels, retail campaigns, supermarkets, exhibitions, and events.

Supported by a highly skilled and certified team, we are committed to delivering consistent quality, reliable supply, and products that meet stringent operational standards. Our manufacturing expertise, attention to detail, and customer-centric approach enable us to tailor solutions in line with each client’s specific requirements.
